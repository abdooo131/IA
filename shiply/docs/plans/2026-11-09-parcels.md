# Shiply Parcels Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The parcels module and the Shiply app: parcels with their address, route and price; pickups; the full network flow with scans, bags and night cars at the sorting facility; receive, sort, dispatch, deliver, fail and return at the last mile hub; cash and fees announced to money; the office screens and the phone screens for hub staff and couriers.

**Architecture:** Module `parcels` with its own schema. It asks location where an address is and which zone, hub and facility serve it (`LocationService.resolve`, `serviceFor`), asks merchants for the price (`MerchantsService.parcelPrice`, new `parcelTerms`), and asks money for the courier's cash holder (`CashService.holderFor`). It announces `cash.collected`, `fee.charged`, `parcel.status_changed` and `parcel.gap_found` through the outbox. Every physical step is one scan row; status changes only through the transition table.

**Tech Stack:** as before.

**Spec:** `shiply/docs/UI.md` (Notes for the Shiply app, Step 3d, Step 3g changes, Parcel flow through the network, Step 4 items 1 and 3), `shiply/docs/TECH.md` sections 3, 4 and 10.6.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in the earlier plans' constraints still applies (integer piastres, `@Inject` everywhere, one owner per data, outbox events with idempotency keys, Arabic and English texts, no hyphens in user facing copy).
- Statuses (kept from the old system): `created, picked_up, at_inbound, sorted, at_outbound, in_transit, at_hub, on_hold, out_for_delivery, attempt_failed, delivered, returned, cancelled, lost`. Changes only through `TRANSITIONS` in `parcels/status.ts`; anything else is refused with `wrong_status`.
- A parcel has `direction: forward | return`. Returns go through the same steps in reverse, in bags, back to the merchant's sorting facility.
- Every scan carries a `deviceScanId` from the device; the same `deviceScanId` twice is answered with the first answer and changes nothing (scans are queued on the phone when the signal drops).
- A scan is accepted only at a site the user belongs to (`UserSite`), with `shiply` use. Couriers are users of kind `agent_person` and see only their own pickups and run.
- Bag capacity is the setting `parcels.bag_capacity` (default 150). A parcel too big for a bag is a bag of one.
- Missing and extra parcels and broken seals become gaps (`parcel.gap_found`), naming the last person who scanned the parcel; CS and QC turns them into cases when it exists.
- Cash: on delivery the courier's holder (`holderFor('courier', userId)`, company `shiply`) gets `cash.collected` for the COD; fees go out as `fee.charged` (company `shiply`).

## Review Focus

1. A scan sent twice (offline queue replay, double tap): one effect, the same answer.
2. A parcel scanned at a step it is not ready for (sorting before inbound, dispatch of a parcel of another hub): red answer with the reason, nothing changes.
3. Two people closing the same bag or confirming the same car at once: one wins, the other gets `wrong_status`.
4. A parcel whose address is unclear: it is created, waits in the needs location queue, and gets its route and price when the area is picked (`location.resolved`).
5. A delivered parcel's COD and fees booked once even if the courier taps Deliver twice.

### Task 1: Parcels, statuses, route and price

**Files:** schema `parcels` in `packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma` + migration; `apps/api/src/parcels/{index.ts, parcels.module.ts, status.ts, tracking.ts, parcels.service.ts, parcels.controller.ts}`; `apps/api/src/merchants/merchants.service.ts` (`parcelTerms`); `app.ts` wiring; test `apps/api/test/parcels.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Models (schema `parcels`): `Parcel(id, tracking unique, merchantId, merchantRef?, customerName, phone, addressText, decisionId?, placeId?, zoneId?, hubId?, facilityId?, originFacilityId?, size, codAmount Int, price Int?, openPackage Bool, notes?, status, direction, attempts Int 0, courierId?, runId?, bagId?, createdById, createdAt, updatedAt)`; `ParcelEvent(id, parcelId, status, note?, userId?, siteId?, at)` (the timeline).
- `tracking.ts`: `newTracking(): string` → `SP` + 9 digits + 1 check digit (Luhn); `isTracking(code)`.
- `status.ts`: `TRANSITIONS: Record<Status, Status[]>`; `canMove(from, to): boolean`.
- `ParcelsService.create(tx, input, userId)`: resolves the address with `LocationService.resolve(tx, {service:'shiply', refType:'parcel', refId, text, phone})`; when placed, sets `placeId`, the route from `serviceFor(placeId)` (zone, hub, facility) and `price` from `parcelPrice`; `originFacilityId` from the merchant's pickup place. Unclear address: created without route or price.
- `ParcelsService.move(tx, parcelId, to, {userId, siteId?, note?})`: checks `canMove`, writes `ParcelEvent`, publishes `parcel.status_changed {parcelId, merchantId, from, to}`.
- Handler `location.resolved` (refType `parcel`): fills place, route and price.
- `MerchantsService.parcelTerms(merchantId) → {codFeeBp, returnFeeBp, openPackageFee, deliveryAttempts}` from the active contract version (defaults when none).
- Routes: `POST /parcels` (shiply use), `GET /parcels?status&hub&merchant&courier&from&to&q&page`, `GET /parcels/:idOrTracking` (with timeline and scans), `POST /parcels/:id/cancel` (only `created`).

- [ ] Tests: created with a clear address → route (zone, hub, facility) and price set, tracking valid; unclear address → no route, then `location.resolved` fills it; `move` refuses `created → delivered` with `wrong_status`; cancel after pickup → 409; `newTracking` check digit catches a single changed digit.
- [ ] RED → implement → GREEN → commit `feat(parcels): parcels, statuses, route and price`.

### Task 2: Scans and pickups

**Files:** `apps/api/src/parcels/{scan.service.ts, scan.controller.ts, pickups.service.ts, pickups.controller.ts}`; test `apps/api/test/pickups.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Models: `Scan(id, deviceScanId unique, step, parcelId?, bagId?, siteId?, userId, ok Bool, reason?, answer Json, at, clientAt?)`; `Pickup(id, merchantId, date, expectedCount Int?, courierId?, status: scheduled|assigned|on_the_way|done|cancelled, recurringId?, createdById)`; `RecurringPickup(id, merchantId, weekdays Int[], active)`.
- `ScanService.scan(input: {step, code, siteId?, deviceScanId, clientAt?, bagSeal?, runId?, pickupId?}, user) → ScanAnswer {ok, colour: 'green'|'amber'|'red', big: string, small?: string, reason?: string, parcelId?}`. Looks up `deviceScanId` first and returns the stored answer. Each step has a handler registered by the later tasks: `registerStep(step, handler)`. Unknown code → red `not_found`.
- Step `pickup` (courier at the merchant): parcel `created` of that pickup's merchant → `picked_up`, `pickup.status` → `on_the_way`.
- Routes: `POST /parcels/scan` (one route for every step); `GET/POST /parcels/pickups`, `POST /parcels/pickups/:id/assign {courierId}`, `POST /parcels/pickups/:id/cancel`; recurring pickups `GET/POST/PATCH /parcels/pickups/recurring`, and `createRecurringFor(date)` (makes the day's pickups once); courier `GET /parcels/me/pickups`.

- [ ] Tests: the same `deviceScanId` twice → one scan row, same answer; a parcel of another merchant at this pickup → red `other_merchant`; pickup scan moves the parcel to `picked_up`; a courier cannot see another courier's pickups; recurring pickups for a date made once even if run twice.
- [ ] RED → implement → GREEN → commit `feat(parcels): scans and pickups`.

### Task 3: Sorting facility (inbound, sorting, outbound bags, night car)

**Files:** `apps/api/src/parcels/{facility.service.ts, facility.controller.ts, bags.service.ts, gaps.service.ts}`; test `apps/api/test/facility.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Models: `Bag(id, seal unique, fromSiteId, toSiteId, direction, status: open|closed|loaded|received|opened, single Bool, carId?, count Int, closedById?, receivedById?)`; `Car(id, fromSiteId, toSiteId, driverName, status: loading|departed|arrived, driverCount Int?, departedAt?, arrivedAt?)`; `Gap(id, siteId, kind: missing|extra|not_handed_over|seal_broken|count_mismatch, step, parcelId?, bagId?, carId?, lastScanById?, open Bool, note?, createdAt, resolvedById?, resolvedAt?)`.
- Steps: `inbound` (`picked_up` → `at_inbound`, at the parcel's origin facility; a parcel not expected on any pickup is amber `extra` and a gap), `sorting` (`at_inbound` → `sorted`; answer `big` = destination hub code; a parcel with no route is amber `no_route` and stays), `outbound` (with `bagSeal`: opens the bag on first use for the parcel's hub; refuses a parcel for another hub (`other_hub`), a full bag (`bag_full`), a parcel not `sorted`; `sorted` → `at_outbound`), `car_load` (code is a bag seal; bag `closed` → `loaded`; parcels → `in_transit` when the car departs).
- `GET /parcels/facility/:siteId/inbound`: per pickup courier ("car") and per merchant, expected against received. `POST /parcels/facility/:siteId/inbound/close {pickupId}`: expected but not received → gap `missing`.
- `GET /parcels/facility/:siteId/stuck`: received by inbound but not sorted, sorted but not bagged, older than the setting `parcels.stuck_minutes` (default 60).
- Bags: `POST /parcels/bags/:seal/close` (once; count stored), `POST /parcels/bags/single {parcelId, seal}` (a bag of one).
- Cars: `POST /parcels/cars {fromSiteId, toSiteId, driverName}`, `POST /parcels/cars/:id/depart {driverCount}` (driver count ≠ loaded bags → 409 `count_mismatch`, nothing departs).
- `GapsService.open(tx, gap)` publishes `parcel.gap_found`; `GET /parcels/gaps?site&open`, `POST /parcels/gaps/:id/resolve {note}`; Home attention `home.parcelGaps` for shiply manage.

- [ ] Tests: inbound close with one parcel missing → one gap naming the courier who picked it up; sorting before inbound → red `wrong_status`; outbound refuses another hub's parcel; bag capacity respected (setting 2 → third parcel `bag_full`); two closes of the same bag at once → one 200, one 409; car departs only with the right driver count and moves parcels to `in_transit`.
- [ ] RED → implement → GREEN → commit `feat(parcels): sorting facility flow`.

### Task 4: Last mile hub, couriers, delivery and returns

**Files:** `apps/api/src/parcels/{hub.service.ts, hub.controller.ts, runs.service.ts, courier.controller.ts, returns.service.ts}`; test `apps/api/test/hub.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Models: `CourierZone(userId, zoneId)` (until Agents owns people); `Run(id, hubId, courierId, date, status: open|out|closed, confirmedById, createdAt)`; `Attempt(id, parcelId, runId, number, result: delivered|failed, reason?, cashCollected Int, proof: otp|photo|none, at)`.
- Steps: `hub_bag` (bag seal at the bag's destination hub: `loaded` → `received`; a bag never loaded → gap `extra`), `hub_receive` (parcel by parcel from an opened bag: `in_transit` → `at_hub`; closing the bag's receive (`POST /parcels/hub/bags/:seal/done`) makes gaps `missing` for parcels in the bag not scanned and `extra` for parcels scanned but not in it), `hub_sort` (answer `big` = zone code, `small` = shelf, which is the zone code), `dispatch` (with `runId`: parcel `at_hub` or `attempt_failed` of this hub → `out_for_delivery`, sets `courierId`, `runId`; another hub's parcel → red `other_hub`).
- Runs: `GET /parcels/hub/:hubId/suggest?zone` (couriers whose `CourierZone` covers the zone, fewest open parcels first); `POST /parcels/runs {hubId, courierId}` (supervisor confirms; one open run per courier a day); `POST /parcels/runs/:id/out`.
- Courier (`agent_person`, own run only): `GET /parcels/me/run`; `POST /parcels/me/parcels/:id/deliver {deviceScanId, cash, proof}` (cash must equal COD, else 400 `wrong_cash`); `POST /parcels/me/parcels/:id/fail {deviceScanId, reason}` (reasons: `no_answer, refused, wrong_address, rescheduled, closed, other`).
- On deliver (one transaction, once per parcel): `delivered`; publishes `cash.collected {companyKey:'shiply', holderId, merchantId, amount: codAmount, sourceType:'parcel', sourceId}` when COD > 0; `fee.charged` delivery (`price`), `cod` (`codAmount × codFeeBp`), `open_package` (when `openPackage`).
- On fail: `attempt_failed`, `attempts + 1`; when `attempts` reaches `deliveryAttempts`, `on_hold` and `direction = return`.
- Returns: step `hub_return` (end of day: the courier's undelivered parcels scanned back: `out_for_delivery` → `at_hub` or, for returns, kept for the return bag); return bags reuse `outbound` and `car_load` from the hub to the merchant's origin facility; at the facility `hub_bag`/`hub_receive` work the same; step `return_handover` (pickup courier gives it back to the merchant): → `returned`, publishes `fee.charged` return (`price × returnFeeBp`).
- End of day cash: the run's close (`POST /parcels/runs/:id/close`) is refused while the courier still holds parcels not scanned back; cash handover itself is the money handover screen.

- [ ] Tests: hub bag done with one parcel missing → gap; dispatch of another hub's parcel → red; the suggested courier is the one covering the zone; deliver twice → one `cash.collected`, one set of fees, COD held by the courier's holder in money; third failed attempt with `deliveryAttempts` 3 → `on_hold`, direction return; return handover → `returned` and a return fee.
- [ ] RED → implement → GREEN → commit `feat(parcels): last mile hub, delivery and returns`.

### Task 6: Phone screens (hub station and courier)
