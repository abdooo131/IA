# Standard operating procedures

One guide per role, in English and Arabic. The same text is shown in the app: every user opens it from the settings menu, Help.
Edit `scripts/sop/content.py`, then run `python3 scripts/sop/build.py` to update the app and these files.

- [Dark store staff](store_staff.md): Receivers, pickers and packers in a dark store or the hub. Your store opens from Home after you sign in.
- [Rider](rider.md): Riders delivering 60 minute orders from one dark store.
- [Merchant](merchant.md): Shops selling with Shiply Now 60 minute delivery.
- [Finance](finance.md): The finance team and the accountant. Everything is in Finance.
- [Admin](admin.md): The team running operations. Home shows the apps your job role opens; the owner opens everything.
- [Sales reps and sales managers](sales.md): Sales reps follow their own leads from the first call to the signed proposal; sales managers give out leads, approve discounts and commission and set targets. Open the Sales app from Home.
