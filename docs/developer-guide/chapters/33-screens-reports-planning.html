Screens: Reporting and Planning

This chapter walks through every screen of the Accounting app's Reporting and Planning menus exactly as a user sees them, plus the journal entry page and the approval request page that the reports lead to. For each screen it gives the address, the menu path, who may open it and which buttons need more rights, a picture of the whole screen, a table of every control, and pictures of the important states. The numbers behind the reports (how a profit is summed, how the forecast and budgets compute) are explained in the chapter Taxes, reports, reconciliation and closing; this chapter is about the screens.

All screens here need Accounting view to open (every GET route of the API defaults to view). Anything that writes needs Accounting use; approving a forecast, opening it again, writing it into the budget and deciding an approval request need Accounting manage. People limited to some stores see only the lines and boxes of their stores. The pictures use the throwaway demo database; the demo people (for example the finance user and the second approver) are fictional.

Searching and filtering the journal

There are four ways to find an entry: the Accounting top bar search, the journal list's search and filters, a report's drill down, and the smart buttons of other documents.

1. The top bar search (every Accounting page)

GET /finance/search?q= (packages/db/src/finance/search.ts). A document number is read by parseDocNumber (packages/shared/src/documents.ts) in loose forms: the prefix in any case, then spaces, a hyphen or # or nothing, leading zeros optional. "JV-002577", "jv 2577", "JV2577" and "jv-#2577" all find JV-002577. Prefixes: JV journal entry, PAY payout, CH cash handover, BILL bill, INV invoice, CN credit note, DN debit note, PO purchase order, CHQ cheque, PC petty cash, RW rider withdrawal, BS bank statement, EC expense claim, BD Basata deposit. Other text finds order numbers (SN…) and merchant or supplier names. People limited to some stores or merchants find only theirs. A result shows the number, its kind and a detail line; Enter or a click opens it; the panel ends with "All reports".

2. The journal list

The search box matches, for each entry: the number (JV-00xxxx), the memo, the order number, the kind key (for example expense, order_fee), the account codes of its lines (so "1101" finds every entry touching CIB) and the name of the person who prepared it. The state lives in the address: q, f (always written, so removing the last filter does not bring the default back), g, sort, desc, page, size.

3. The ledger lines drill list

From any report line (see the drill down report page above) the ledger lines list adds what the journal lacks: a day range (from, to), Store (including No store), Merchant (including No merchant), Rider, Kind of entry and Cost tag pickers, and the filters Debits, Credits, Deliveries and fees, Cash, Payouts, Bills, Journal entries by hand, With an order, No store, grouped by Month, Day, Store, Merchant, Rider, Kind, Account or Cost tag. Each row opens its journal entry.

4. Smart buttons of other documents

The same page for other kinds of entry

Journal entry status

An entry is written to the books only when it is posted. Posting (post() in packages/db/src/finance/ledger.ts) inserts the entry and its lines in one transaction; the lines must add up to zero (a deferred trigger refuses anything else) and the rows can never be changed afterwards. That is why the entry page always shows the pill Posted and nothing else.

Money that needs a second person

Money going out from finance.approval_threshold_piastres (10,000 EGP by default) and every claimed money in (while finance.approve_money_in is 1) is not posted when it is asked. It is stored as a finance request (finance_requests) and waits in Money in or Dashboard, Approvals. Nothing appears in the journal, the reports or the balances until a second person approves it.

Closed books

Once the books are closed through a day (books_lock.locked_before), post() and a database trigger refuse any line dated before the lock: the forms answer "This date is in a closed month. Choose a later date, or ask an admin to reopen the month." (books_closed). Entries already in the closed days stay readable and can still receive notes, reminders and attachments, but no new lines; a correction is posted on an open day. Jobs that would date a line inside closed books move it to the first open day (accrual reversals, storage catch up, depreciation).
