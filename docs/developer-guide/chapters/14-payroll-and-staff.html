14. Payroll and staff costs

This chapter explains how a month's salaries are worked out and booked: salary packages kept by HR, Egyptian social insurance, the martyrs fund and salary income tax computed from settings, advances, bonuses, sales commission, unpaid leave and violation deductions, outsourced staff billed by their company, and payslips. All arithmetic is in integer piastres. The deduction helper is the same half up share used everywhere:

share(amount, bp) = floor((amount * bp + 5000) / 10000)

14.2 Salary packages

HR keeps salary packages (salary_packages with salary_allowances): from a day, a basic salary, an insured wage, and allowances (kind, label, amount). History is kept. Accounting never edits them.

packageInForce(employee, day) = the package with the latest effectiveFrom <= day,
                                else (before the first package) the first package
packageGross(package)         = basic + sum(allowances)

The run reads the package in force on the month's last day (packages/db/src/hr-pay.ts).

14.4 The payslip calculation

Pure functions in packages/shared/src/payroll.ts; the run is previewPayroll() then runPayroll() in packages/db/src/finance/payroll.ts.

base       = line.grossPiastres given by finance, else packageGross(package in force on the month's last day)
bonus      = taxed bonus entered for the month (overtime, target bonus)
commission = sum of approved, unpaid sales commissions of months <= this month
deduction  = penalties or unpaid days entered by finance
hrItems    = unpaid leave days and final violation deductions HR hands over (14.8)
gross      = base + bonus + commission − deduction − hrItems      (refused when negative)

insured    = min(max(package.insuredWage ?? gross, siMinWage), siMaxWage)
siEmployee = gross > 0 ? share(insured, siEmployeeBp) : 0
siEmployer = gross > 0 ? share(insured, siEmployerBp) : 0
martyrs    = share(gross, martyrsBp)

yearlyTaxable = (gross − siEmployee) * 12 − personalExemption
incomeTax     = floor((yearlyIncomeTax(yearlyTaxable) + 6) / 12)     (the yearly tax / 12, half up)
afterTax      = gross − siEmployee − martyrs − incomeTax
net           = afterTax − advanceRecovery                            (refused when negative)

Yearly income tax

yearlyIncomeTax(taxable):
    if taxable <= 0: return 0
    rates = band rates
    skipped = 0
    for k in 0..4: if skipFrom[k] > 0 and taxable > skipFrom[k]: skipped = k + 1
    for i in 0..skipped−1: rates[i] = rates[skipped] (or the top rate)
    tax = 0; lower = 0
    for each band i: if taxable <= lower: stop
                     tax += share(min(taxable, band.to) − lower, rates[i]); lower = band.to
    if taxable > lower: tax += share(taxable − lower, topBp)
    return tax

Each band's tax is rounded half up to the piastre on its own.

High earner check: yearly taxable 650,000 EGP (65,000,000). It passes the first threshold only, so band 1 is taxed at 10%:

  • band 1: 4,000,000 × 10% = 400,000
  • band 2: 1,500,000 × 10% = 150,000
  • band 3: 1,500,000 × 15% = 225,000
  • band 4: 13,000,000 × 20% = 2,600,000
  • band 5: 20,000,000 × 22.5% = 4,500,000
  • band 6: 25,000,000 × 25% = 6,250,000

Yearly tax 14,125,000 piastres (141,250 EGP).

14.5 The salary run and its posting

submitPayroll() checks everything with previewPayroll() (month not in the future: future_month; each person once; salary set: salary_not_set; recovery not above the advance owed: more_than_advance), then posts at once below finance.approval_threshold_piastres or waits for a second person (the amount compared is the total net). The run happens once per month: payroll_already_run. The money account (bank or box) is locked and must hold the total net.

The entry is dated the month's last day at 23:00 Cairo when the month is over, else now (the current month may be paid while it runs). One entry for everyone:

In the same transaction the HR items are marked as taken (markHrPayrollItems(), each once) and the commissions are marked paid. The taxes owed are paid later from Taxes (chapter 15).

14.7 Sales commission in payroll

Sales commission is computed by the Sales app (packages/db/src/crm-sales.ts, packages/shared/src/crm-sales.ts). Once a Cairo month is over, for each sales rep (key commission:<rep>:<month>):

counted fees = fees before VAT (revenue lines tagged with the merchant) of the rep's qualified won
               merchants inside their commission months
hit        = a fees target above 0 exists and counted fees >= target
commission = hit ? sum over deals of commissionOn(dealFees, deal.commission_bp) : 0
commissionOn(fees, bp) = share(max(0, fees), bp)

A hit is a draft approved (or adjusted with a reason) by another sales manager. Approved commissions of months up to the run's month (payableCommissions()) are added to the employee's gross in the salary run, taxed; outsourced reps are paid by a direct staff bonus.

14.8 Unpaid leave and violation deductions

hrPayrollItems() in packages/db/src/hr-inbox.ts hands the run, for an employee, every unpaid leave day and every final violation deduction up to the month's last day that no run has taken:

dayDeduction(basic, days, perMonth) = round(basic * days / perMonth)    (perMonth = hr.payroll_days_per_month)
unpaid leave day item  = dayDeduction(basic, day fraction, perMonth)
violation item         = penalty deduction_amount ? violation.amountPiastres
                                                  : dayDeduction(basic, violation.days, perMonth)

They reduce the gross before tax. Outsourced people's deductions are only listed for their company. Riders paid per delivery are charged to their wallet instead (chapter 11).

14.9 Outsourced staff

An outsourced person's month is a supplier bill from their own outsourcing company (chapter 12) naming the person: the cost line carries the user, store and rider tags, so the person's cost appears in profit per store and merchant profit. Paying it can take back their advance (Cr 1500). The bill's expense account is the one chosen (for example 5402 Salaries and wages).

14.10 Payslips

Employees on the salary run see only their own payslips in My HR (myPayslips(), myPayslip()), read from the posted payroll:<month> entry; printable in English or Arabic; a payslip_ready push tells them. Riders paid per delivery see their wallet instead.
