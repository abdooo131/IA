Screens: Dashboard and Money in

This chapter walks through the Accounting app exactly as a finance user sees it, from the app tile to the last screen of the Money in menu, and then shows the merchant's side of the same payout. Every screenshot was taken on the local demo copy on 11/11/2026 with two finance users: the preparer (accounting use) and a second finance user (accounting manage) who approves and confirms. Every action shown was really performed, so the results (entries, numbers, statuses) are the system's own answers.

Access words used below. View: may open the screen and read. Use: may prepare, upload, mark paid, register and cancel. Manage: may also approve what waits for a second person (payouts above the limit, Approvals requests, bulk approve). Every route of the Accounting API is guarded by @Access('accounting'); reading needs view, writing needs use, and the routes listed as manage carry @Access('accounting', 'manage') (requests/:id/decide, requests/bulk-decide, payouts/:id/approve, payouts/bulk-approve, payout checks setup). On top of the level, the second person rule is checked by the server: nobody approves what they asked for, and the person who confirmed the statement and slip of a payout cannot be the one who marks it paid.
VAT shows 0.00 on every fee, invoice and note in these screenshots because the demo database holds fee.vat_bp = 0. With the owner's 14% the same screens show the VAT column filled.

The frozen Statement of Account

The SOA is made by freezePayoutStatement in the same transaction as the payout (numbers SOA-000001 onward, gapless under an advisory lock) from the merchant's own statement: from the previous paid payout's cutoff to this cutoff, per order and by kind with the VAT inside each fee. It never changes afterwards; the PDF says so on its first lines.

Uploading the Proof of Transfer and its refusals

The system compares the amount typed from the slip with the payout; it does not read the image. In the demo the Nile Pantry slip picture shows another amount than the one typed: the second person check exists exactly to catch that, by a person opening the slip.
