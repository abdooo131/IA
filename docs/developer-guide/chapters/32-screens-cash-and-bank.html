Screens: Cash and bank

This chapter walks through every screen of the Cash and bank menu of the Accounting app, then the two rider screens and the two store screens that feed it. Every screenshot was taken on the local demo copy (Cairo time 11 Nov 2026, about 23:00) after performing the actions shown, so each figure also shows the real result of the action. The money rules behind these screens (accounts, postings, ages, deadlines) are explained in chapter 11, Cash on delivery, riders, store boxes and rider pay, and in chapter 12, Payables and expenses (sections Loans and Bank accounts in another currency). The red numbered circles on the screenshots were added for this guide; each figure with circles is followed by a legend table.

Access in one line: every page of this menu opens with Accounting view (GET routes). Every button that changes something needs Accounting use (POST routes default to use in AccessGuard), except where a row says otherwise. People limited to some stores see only the riders, boxes, handovers and Basata deposits of their stores; banks, merchants, bills and loans stay company wide.

The menu path for all Accounting screens below is Accounting app tile, top bar menu Cash and bank. The orange counter beside Cash and bank in the top bar adds up the items waiting on the pages of that menu (open bank lines, Basata deposits waiting, money movements without a receipt and so on), so it changes while you work.

Cash position

Address /finance/cash-position. Menu: Cash and bank, Cash position. Opens with Accounting view; there is no button that changes data on this page. It answers one question: where is the money right now. Data comes from GET /finance/cash-position (function cashPosition in packages/db/src/finance/cash-control.ts), the CSV from GET /finance/cash-position/export.csv.
