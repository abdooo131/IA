12. Expenses, suppliers, bills and the assets we pay for

This chapter covers money going out to suppliers and for the company's own costs: expenses paid at once, supplier bills with withholding tax, bills in another currency, cheques, purchase orders, packaging stock, recurring expenses, prepayments, equipment and depreciation, accruals, loans, cost tags, bank accounts in another currency and expense claims. Amounts are integer piastres.

12.1 Rules shared by every movement of money

Money accounts

Money leaves or enters only through banks (group 1100, CIB is 1101) and cash boxes (group 1200, store boxes included). Before money leaves, the account row is locked (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) and its balance read, so two payments at the same moment are checked one after the other. Most movements use mustHold(tx, account, amount, at) in packages/db/src/finance/books.ts:

running balance of the account at each distinct moment (lines at one moment count together)
atBalance = running balance at the movement's own moment (latest moment <= at)
laterMin  = lowest running balance at any moment after it (atBalance when none)
refuse not_enough_money when amount > min(atBalance, laterMin)

A backdated payment therefore never turns an earlier or a later balance negative. A moment inside closed books answers books_closed first.

Dates

eventTime(day): no day or today means now; a past Cairo day is booked at its noon (Cairo time); a future day is refused (future_date).

The second person rule

submit() in packages/db/src/finance/controls.ts either posts at once or stores a finance_requests row that another person decides (decideFinanceRequest(); nobody decides their own request: same_person; a rejection needs a note).

Request kindsWhen it waits
capital_in, other_income, merchant_receipt, merchant_adjustment, cheque_in, deposit_in, loan_in, debit_note, asset_disposal, and deposit_out through the merchant's walletalways, while finance.approve_money_in is 1 (default 1)
credit_notealways, whatever the setting
everything else (expenses, drawings, bill payments, tax payments, prepayments, asset purchases, packaging purchases, advances, bonuses, payroll, loan payments, issued cheques, journal entries moving money out)when the amount is at least finance.approval_threshold_piastres (default 1,000,000 = 10,000 EGP; 0 turns it off)

The request keeps the client's idempotency key; one request per key exists while it is pending or approved (a rejected one can be sent again). When approved, the same posting function runs with its own ledger key, so it still posts once.

12.5 Paying a bill and withholding tax

payBill(), through submitBillPayment() (second person from the limit). A bill can be paid in several payments. The amount entered is how much of the bill the payment settles, withholding tax included.

Withholding exact to the piastre

Withholding is a share of the amount before VAT. It is computed on the running total paid, so the payments together always withhold exactly round(net × whtBp / 10,000) once the bill is fully paid:

withheldAt(paid) = round(net * whtBp * paid / (10000 * total))      (Math.round)
wht(this payment) = withheldAt(paidBefore + settled) − withheldAt(paidBefore)
cash = paid − wht − advanceRecovery − offset          (refused when negative)

Other options: advanceRecoveryPiastres for a bill naming a person (an advance we gave them, taken back from what we pay their company; never more than their 1500 balance), offsetPiastres (what the supplier owes us beyond its open bills, chapter 11), viaChequesPayable (paid by a cheque we wrote: credit 2451 instead of a bank). The bank or box must hold the cash.

AccountDebitCreditTags
2500 supplier walletsettled − offsetstore
5901 exchange differencesfxDiff (when positive)−fxDiff (when negative)
bank, cash box or 2451 cheques payablecash
2302 Withholding tax payablewht
1500 staff advance of the personadvanceRecoveryuser

Idempotency key bill_payment:<clientKey>; kind bill_payment; data stores gross (settled), wht, recovery, offset, fxDiff and net = round(bill.net × settled / bill.total), the amount before VAT used by form 41. The bill becomes paid when paidPiastres reaches the total.

Bill: net 10,000 EGP, VAT 1,400 EGP, total 1,140,000 piastres, withholding 3% (300 bp). Full withholding = 30,000.

  • Payment 1 settles 3,333.33 EGP (333,333): withheldAt(333,333) = round(1,000,000 × 300 × 333,333 / 11,400,000,000) = round(8,771.92) = 8,772. Cash 324,561.
  • Payment 2 settles the remaining 806,667: withheldAt(1,140,000) = 30,000, so wht = 30,000 − 8,772 = 21,228. Cash 785,439.

Withheld in total 30,000 exactly. Each payment: Dr supplier (settled), Cr bank (cash), Cr 2302 (wht).

12.6 Bills in another currency

A bill may be in another currency (bills.currency, three upper case letters). The rate is typed as text and stored exactly as fx_rate_micro = EGP per unit × 1,000,000 (rateMicro(): up to 6 decimals, no float). Foreign amounts are cents.

centsToPiastres(cents, micro) = floor((cents * micro + 500000) / 1000000)     (half up)
bill.net   = centsToPiastres(foreignNetCents, billMicro)
bill.vat   = centsToPiastres(foreignVatCents, billMicro)
foreignTotal = foreignNetCents + foreignVatCents

A payment is entered in the bill's cents at today's rate:

settled = (cents == foreignTotal − foreignPaid) ? total − paidPiastres          (last payment settles exactly)
                                                : centsToPiastres(cents, billMicro)
paid    = centsToPiastres(cents, todayMicro)
          (or, paid from a bank account held in that currency, the cents' book value, 12.14)
fxDiff  = paid − settled      → 5901 exchange differences (loss positive, gain negative)

A USD 1,000.00 bill (100,000 cents, no VAT) at 48.75: net = floor((100,000 × 48,750,000 + 500,000) / 1,000,000) = 4,875,000 piastres. Paid in full when the rate is 49.10 from an EGP bank: paid = 4,910,000; settled = 4,875,000; fxDiff = 35,000. Entry: Dr supplier 4,875,000; Dr 5901 35,000; Cr bank 4,910,000.

12.7 Cheques

The register is packages/db/src/finance/cheques.ts; numbers CHQ-000001 onward (gapless). Purposes: received (merchant balance, security deposit, supplier refund, other income) and issued (a bill, a payout, a deposit paid back).

 received: awaiting_approval ─book─▶ received ─deposit─▶ deposited ─clear─▶ cleared
                                        └────────────────bounce / cancel────▶ bounced / cancelled
 issued:   awaiting_approval ─book─▶ issued ─clear─▶ cleared
                                        └──bounce / cancel──▶ bounced / cancelled

Booking

Registering a received cheque submits a cheque_in request (always reviewed while finance.approve_money_in is 1); an issued cheque submits cheque_out (from the approval limit); a payout cheque books at once (the payout's own approval applies). Until booked the cheque is awaiting_approval; a rejected request cancels it.

Received1151 Cheques under collectionparty: merchant wallet, merchant deposit 2600, supplier wallet, or the income accountcheque:<id>:book, cheque_received
Issued for a billpayBill() with viaChequesPayable: Dr supplier, Cr 2451 Cheques payable (cash part), Cr 2302 (withholding)bill_payment:cheque:<id>, bill_payment
Issued for a payout2100 merchant wallet2451 Cheques payablepayout:<payoutId>, merchant_payout
Issued, deposit paid back2600 merchant deposit2451 Cheques payablecheque:<id>:book, deposit_refunded

For a bill the cheque's amount is the cash part; settledFor() finds the amount of the bill it settles: it starts from round(cash × total / (total − net × whtBp / 10,000)) and walks by single piastres until settled − wht(settled) = cash (refused with cheque_amount_mismatch if no amount fits, for example after a partial payment meanwhile).

Clearing

A received cheque cannot clear before its due day (cheque_not_due); an issued one needs the bank to hold the amount.

DirectionDebitCredit
Receivedbank1151 Cheques under collection
Issued2451 Cheques payablebank

Idempotency key cheque:<id>:clear; kind cheque_cleared. Depositing a received cheque at the bank moves no money (status only).

Bounce or cancel

A bounce is possible while a received cheque is received or deposited, or an issued one is issued (reason of at least 3 characters). A cancel (note of at least 3 characters) is possible while the cheque is awaiting approval (its request is dropped, nothing was posted) or booked but not yet deposited or cleared. Both post the booking entry's lines turned round, with their tags (key cheque:<id>:reverse, kind cheque_bounced or cheque_cancelled). For a bill, its paidPiastres goes down by the amount settled and it is open again; a payout paid by the cheque is cancelled. A bounce raises a cheque_bounced alert. Withholding lists leave out payments whose cheque bounced or was cancelled (chapter 15).

12.9 Packaging stock at average cost

Packaging (bags, boxes, labels, tape) is our own stock, never stock_movements (packages/db/src/packaging.ts): packaging_levels keeps units and value per store and item; packaging_movements is append only (receive, consume, adjust, count). Each movement is written first, then the level changes with a guarded update that never lets units or value go below zero.

valueOut(level, qty) = qty >= level.onHand ? level.value                 (the last unit takes what is left)
                                           : round(level.value * qty / level.onHand)
average cost shown = round(value / onHand)
on receipt: item.unitCostPiastres = round(value / qty)    (used for found units in adjustments)

Bought and paid at once

recordPackagingPurchase() through the second person rule (from the limit): Dr 1402 Packaging stock, Cr bank or box; key packaging_purchase:<clientKey>; kind packaging_purchase; units received at the amount paid.

Used when an order is packed

consumePackagingForOrder() uses the most specific recipe (merchant at the store, merchant, store, everyone) plus the box staff chose. Packing is never blocked: missing units raise one packaging_short alert; what is available is used.

AccountDebitCreditTags
5404 Packaging and suppliessum of valueOutorder, store, merchant
1402 Packaging stocksamesame

Idempotency key packaging:<orderId>; kind packaging_used. The order tag makes the cost appear in merchant profit and cost per order. stock.packaging_low_units (default 50, store override, 0 off) raises one packaging_low alert per store and item. Adjustments post kind packaging_adjustment: lost units leave at average cost, found units come in at the item's unit cost.

A store holds 200 bags valued 3,000 EGP (300,000). An order uses 3 bags: valueOut = round(300,000 × 3 / 200) = 4,500. Dr 5404 4,500; Cr 1402 4,500. The level becomes 197 bags, 295,500.

12.10 Recurring expenses

packages/db/src/finance/recurring.ts. A recurring expense (rent, internet, software) is entered once: category, money account, amount, optional VAT, store, memo, reference, day of month 1 to 28, start month and optional end month. Once its day of the month has come (the daily job checks today >= dayOfMonth, so a late run still catches it), the job runRecurring() prepares that month's expense as a request, always queued for a second person (never posted by itself) with idempotency key recurring:<id>:<YYYY-MM> and the day set to that month's day. On approval it posts as an ordinary expense (key expense:recurring:<id>:<YYYY-MM>, kind expense).

12.14 Bank accounts in another currency

packages/db/src/finance/fx.ts. The books stay in EGP; each line on a USD or EUR bank account carries its EGP amount plus foreign_cents (signed like the line) and the rate used (fx_rate_micro). post() refuses a line on such an account without its foreign amount (foreign_currency_account), so payouts, withdrawals and petty cash can never use one. Each account has two balances: EGP (the books) and cents (the bank).

bookValueOut(account, cents) = cents == heldCents ? egpBalance
                             : floor((egpBalance * cents + floor(heldCents / 2)) / heldCents)
impliedRateMicro(piastres, cents) = floor((|piastres| * 1000000 + |cents| / 2) / |cents|)
MovementLines
Buy currencyDr foreign bank (EGP paid, +cents), Cr EGP bank
Sell currencyDr EGP bank (EGP received), Dr or Cr 5901 (book value − received), Cr foreign bank (book value, −cents)
Foreign to foreign (same currency)Dr receiving bank (book value, +cents), Cr sending bank (book value, −cents)
Pay a bill in that currencyDr supplier (bill rate), Dr or Cr 5901, Cr foreign bank (book value, −cents); no withholding or advance recovery allowed
Month end revaluationDr or Cr foreign bank (0 cents), Cr or Dr 5901

Transfers use key transfer:<clientKey>, kind money_transfer. Revaluation, once per account and month that is over, at the month's last millisecond:

target = floor((foreignCentsAtMonthEnd * rateMicro + 500000) / 1000000)

Key fx_reval:<accountId>:<YYYY-MM>; kind fx_revaluation.

A USD account holds 50,000.00 USD (5,000,000 cents) booked at 2,430,000 EGP (243,000,000 piastres). At month end the rate is 48.90: target = floor((5,000,000 × 48,900,000 + 500,000) / 1,000,000) = 244,500,000; diff 1,500,000. Dr USD bank 1,500,000 (0 cents); Cr 5901 1,500,000 (a gain of 15,000 EGP shown as a credit on the expense account).

On approval it gets LN-000001 and its own liability account (group 2700: 2701, 2702...). The money comes into a bank (group 1100 only). Money in waits for a second person (loan_in).

AccountDebitCreditTags
bankprincipal
27nn the loanprincipal

Key loan:<clientKey>; kind loan_received.

The schedule (whole piastres, worked out once)

Monthly rate r = bp / 120,000. mulDivRound(a, b, c) = (2ab + c) / (2c) in BigInt, which is round half up of a × b / c.

declining (equal instalment, annuity):
    A = (120000 + bp)^n ;  B = 120000^n
    instalment = mulDivRound(P * bp, A, 120000 * (A − B))     (bp 0: mulDivRound(P, 1, n))
    each month: interest = mulDivRound(remaining, bp, 120000)
                principal = instalment − interest (last month, or when larger than remaining: remaining)
flat:
    totalInterest = mulDivRound(P, bp * n, 120000)
    interestEach  = floor(totalInterest / n); principalEach = floor(P / n)
    last month takes what is left of both
due days: the same day each month; a missing day becomes the month's last day

12.16 Cost tags

Besides the store, merchant, rider and person tags every line carries, accounting manage may open cost tags (a campaign, a project).
