15. Taxes, reports, reconciliation and closing

This chapter explains how the tax worksheets are built from the ledger (VAT, withholding, salary tax and insurance, company income tax), how tax is paid, how the financial statements and drill down reports compute and compare, how profit per store and the fully loaded cost per order share costs, how cash flow and the four week forecast work, how bank lines are matched, what the month end checklist checks, how a year is closed, and what the exports contain. Every report reads the ledger lines; nothing is stored as a balance.

15.1 Periods and Cairo time

Reports take Cairo calendar days. A day D covers [cairoDayStart(D), cairoDayStart(D + 1)); cairoDayStart() (packages/shared) applies Egypt's summer time, never a fixed offset. In SQL a moment's Cairo day or month is always taken as (occurred_at AT TIME ZONE 'UTC') AT TIME ZONE 'Africa/Cairo'. Drill down reports refuse ranges longer than 400 days (range_too_long).

15.2 VAT report (per month of a year)

vatReport(db, year) in packages/db/src/finance/period.ts reads every line on 2301 VAT payable in the year, by Cairo month. Columns follow the entry's kind, not only the line's sign:

purchase kinds = supplier_bill, supplier_bill_cancel, expense, expense_claim
output = −sum(lines) where kind = credit_note
                        or (kind not tax_payment, not a purchase kind, and line < 0)
input  =  sum(lines) where kind is a purchase kind
                        or (kind not tax_payment, not credit_note, not a purchase kind, and line > 0)
paid   =  sum(lines) where kind = tax_payment
due    = output − input

So a credit note lowers output, a cancelled bill lowers input, a debit note raises output, a rider office bill (kind supplier_bill) is input, and other entries (journal entries) go by sign.

15.3 Monthly VAT return

vatReturn(db, month) in packages/db/src/finance/returns.ts builds the return worksheet from the entries of the month that have a 2301 line (year close and tax payments excluded), grouped by kind:

for each kind: revenue = −sum(4101 lines); vat = −sum(2301 lines)
               expense = sum(lines on expense accounts, 2401 goods received and 2402 commission accrued)
credit_note        : creditNotes += −revenue ; creditNoteVat += −vat
purchase kinds     : purchases += expense + sum(data.onWalletPiastres) ; inputVat += −vat
revenue ≠ 0 or vat > 0 (fees, minimum, debit notes...):
                     sales += revenue ; outputVat += vat
                     (debit notes also shown apart as "of which")
otherwise          : purchases += expense ; inputVat += −vat
carriedIn = max(0, balance of 2301 before the month)        (a debit balance is credit carried in)
due       = outputVat − creditNoteVat − inputVat − carriedIn
duePiastres = max(due, 0) ; carriedOut = due < 0 ? −due : 0
paidSince = VAT tax payments booked after the month

A rider office's commission since 11 November 2026 is booked on the office wallet at each delivery, so its month end bill entry carries only the VAT; onWalletPiastres in the bill entry's data adds the commission back to purchases (counted once, since the entry has one 2301 line).

November: fees 400,000 EGP with VAT 56,000; one credit note of 1,000 EGP with VAT 140; bills and expenses with VAT claimed 9,000; a debit balance of 2,000 carried in from October. Due = 56,000 − 140 − 9,000 − 2,000 = 44,860 EGP; nothing carried out.

The month end checklist has a hand ticked item for the VAT return (15.15).

15.4 Withholding tax, form 41

withholdingPayments(db, { year, quarter }) lists every bill payment of the quarter that kept withholding tax, one row per payment, leaving out payments made by a cheque that later bounced or was cancelled (reversedPaymentIds()):

row: day, voucher JV number, supplier, tax id,
     net (amount before VAT of the payment = data.net = round(bill.net * settled / bill.total)),
     rate = net ? round(wht * 10000 / net) : supplier.whtBp,
     withheld = data.wht

The CSV (form41-<year>-Q<n>.csv) has the columns: Tax registration number, Name, Date, Amount before VAT, Rate % (two decimals), Withheld, Voucher. whtReport() in payables.ts gives the same quarter per supplier, flagging suppliers with withholding and no tax id.

15.5 Salary tax and social insurance lists

payrollForms(db, month) reads the month's payroll:<month> entry (chapter 14) and joins each person's national id and social insurance number from their employee file. Totals: gross, income tax, insured wages, both insurance shares, martyrs fund; missingIds counts people without an id or number. Two CSVs (salaries switch needed): the salary tax list and the social insurance list (Insurance number, National ID, Name, Insured wage, Employee share, Company share, Total).

15.6 Company income tax

corporateTaxEstimate(year):
    pl              = profit and loss of the year (year close entry left out)
    booked          = the 5801 Company income tax line of that year
    profitBeforeTax = pl.profit + booked
    estimate        = profitBeforeTax > 0 ? round(profitBeforeTax * finance.corporate_tax_bp / 10000) : 0
                      (setting default 2250 = 22.5%)

The accountant settles the amount (the taxable profit may differ from accounting profit). bookCorporateTax() books it before the year is closed, only for a year that is over, and only the difference with what is booked:

5801 Company income taxdifference

Idempotency key corporate_tax:<year>:<n> where n is the number of earlier corporate tax entries of the year plus one; kind corporate_tax; dated two milliseconds before the year ends. A lower amount posts a negative difference (the lines turn round).

Profit before tax 2,400,000 EGP: estimate round(240,000,000 × 2,250 / 10,000) = 54,000,000 piastres (540,000 EGP). The accountant settles on 520,000 EGP: Dr 5801 52,000,000, Cr 2306 52,000,000. Changed later to 530,000 EGP: a second entry Dr 5801 1,000,000, Cr 2306 1,000,000.

The tax is one of: VAT (2301), withholding (2302), salary income tax (2303), social insurance (2304, both shares), martyrs fund (2305), company income tax (2306). 15.8 Financial statements

All three are in packages/db/src/finance/books.ts; they sum lines per account (sumsByAccount()) with optional store and merchant filters. A balance is shown "as an accountant reads it": natural(category, debit − credit) keeps the sign for assets and expenses and flips it for liabilities, equity and income.

ReportLines readResult
Trial balanceall lines of the periodper account: debits, credits, balance; total debits always equal total credits (unfiltered)
Profit and lossincome and expense accounts in the period, the year_close entry left outincome, expenses, profit = income − expenses
Balance sheetall lines before the end of the chosen dayassets, liabilities, equity, plus earnings = income − expenses not yet closed into 3401; wallets shown as one line per group (store boxes kept apart)

Accumulated depreciation (1701) is an asset account with a credit balance, so it appears as a negative asset. With a store or merchant filter only the lines tagged with them count, so a filtered report may not balance (the screen says so).

15.9 Drill down and comparisons

drillReport() in packages/db/src/finance/drill.ts runs the same three reports once for the period and once for the comparison, every line keyed by its account; a click opens the ledger lines behind the line (GET /finance/ledger-lines), whose total is the report line exactly. For the balance sheet the earnings drill starts after the last year close.

Comparison periods

comparisonRange(range, mode) in packages/shared/src/report-period.ts:

  • last_year: the same days a year earlier (a day the month lacks becomes its last day).
  • previous, in this order:
    1. a year to date (from 1 January to a day in a later quarter of the same year): the same days a year earlier;
    2. a quarter to date (from a quarter's first day to a later month of that quarter): the quarter before, the same number of days into it, never past that quarter's end (a quarter's last day stays a quarter's last day);
    3. any other range starting on the 1st: moved back by the number of months it covers (a month's last day stays a month's last day);
    4. otherwise: moved back by its own length in days.

15.10 Profit per store and the fully loaded cost per order

profitBy(db, { from, to, by }) in packages/db/src/finance/profit.ts groups income (−sum of revenue category lines) and costs (sum of expense category lines) by the line's store, merchant or rider tag, year_close left out; lines without the tag form one company wide row.

costPerOrder():

orders(s)       = delivered orders of store s in the period (deliveredAt in range)
allOrders       = sum over stores
companyCosts    = costs of the untagged (company wide) row
shared(s)       = allOrders > 0 ? round(companyCosts * orders(s) / allOrders) : 0
incomePerOrder  = round(income(s) / orders(s))
directPerOrder  = round(directCosts(s) / orders(s))
fullCostPerOrder= round((directCosts(s) + shared(s)) / orders(s))
profitPerOrder  = round((income(s) − directCosts(s) − shared(s)) / orders(s))
(null when the store delivered nothing)

Store A delivered 3,000 orders, store B 1,000. Company wide costs 200,000 EGP. A's share = round(20,000,000 × 3,000 / 4,000) = 15,000,000 (150,000 EGP). A's direct costs 240,000 EGP: direct cost per order 80 EGP, fully loaded (240,000 + 150,000) / 3,000 = 130 EGP.

15.11 Cash flow (direct method)

cashFlow(db, { from, to, darkStoreId? }) in period.ts. Money is every line on groups 1100 (banks), 1200 (cash boxes, Money at Basata included) and 1300 (rider cash).

opening = sum of money lines before from ; closing = sum before to
per entry: net = sum of its money lines in the period
per kind : in  = sum of net over entries with net > 0
           out = −sum of net over entries with net < 0
section  : asset_purchase → investing ; capital_in, capital_out → financing ; every other kind → operating

Because each entry is netted first, a move between our own money accounts (a bank deposit from a box, a handover from rider cash to a box, a transfer between banks) nets to 0 and does not appear. Loan entries are operating in the current mapping. With a store, only money lines tagged with that store count.

15.12 Four week cash forecast

cashForecast(db, now) in period.ts:

planned kinds = expense, bill_payment, payroll, tax_payment, staff_advance, prepayment,
                asset_purchase, capital_in, capital_out, other_income
trading       = sum(in − out) of moneyByKind over the last 56 days, planned kinds left out
weeklyTrading = round(trading / 8)
money now     = sum of money lines (1100, 1200, 1300) up to now
for week w = 0..3 (7 days from today):
    bills     = remaining of open bills due in the week (week 0 also takes overdue ones)
    recurring = amount of each active recurring expense whose day of month falls in the week
                (within its start and end months)
    salaries  = (number of month ends in the week) × gross of every package in force today
                for active employees on our payroll
    taxes     = week 0 only: VAT and withholding owed now (never below 0)
    closing   = opening + weeklyTrading − (bills + recurring + salaries + taxes)

15.14 Bank reconciliation

Importing statements

Finance uploads the bank's statement as .xlsx or CSV (read on the server without a spreadsheet library; old .xls is refused with a "save as" hint), picks the columns (guessed from English and Arabic headers; debit and credit columns or one signed amount). Dates and amounts are parsed by parseStatementDate() and parseStatementAmount() (packages/shared/src/statement.ts: day first dates, month names, Excel day numbers; brackets, a minus or DR make an amount negative; no floats). Each upload is a statement BS-000001 onward. Each line gets a fingerprint:

base = day | amount | balance | description | reference
n    = how many times the same base appeared earlier in this file (1, 2...)
fingerprint = sha256(base | n)

Lines whose fingerprint already exists for the account are skipped, so overlapping uploads add nothing, while two equal transfers on one day in one file are both kept. A future day or an amount of 0 is refused.

Automatic matching

autoMatch() in packages/db/src/finance/bank.ts runs after an import and when the screens open. For each unmatched bank line, oldest first, it takes the ledger lines of the same account with exactly the same amount within 3 days that prove no bank line yet; when exactly one is the closest it is matched; two equally close ones are left for a person. Matching never records money.

Suggestions for a person

rankSuggestions(bankLine, openLines) in packages/shared/src/bank-match.ts:

Sorted by rank, then by distance from the bank amount, then by day gap, then by ids; at most 12 shown. Lines picked by hand match when they add up exactly (sumMatches(); the extra lines are kept in bank_line_matches; a trigger lets a ledger line prove only one bank line; reopening removes them). Money out with no entry can be booked as bank charges in one tap (bookBankCharge(): an expense on 5501 Bank charges through the second person rule, key expense:bankcharge:<bankLineId>, then matched); a line that needs no entry is set aside with a reason of at least 5 characters. Money in is never created from a statement line.

Reconciliation view

reconciliation(db, { accountId, day }): book balance at the end of the day, the last bank balance printed on a statement line up to that day, the difference, bank lines not in the books, and book lines not on any statement since the first day a statement covers.

Any item except books_closed can be ticked by hand with a note of at least 3 characters (month_end_marks, audited). The month is ready to close when it is over and every other item is done. Closing (closeBooks() in controls.ts, accounting manage) sets books_lock.closedThrough to a day already over and lockedBefore to the start of the next day; post() and a database trigger then refuse lines before it (books_closed). Moving the date back reopens days: admin only, with a reason of at least 5 characters, audited as finance.books_reopen.

15.16 Closing a year

closeYear(db, { year }) in period.ts (approve level), once per year, years in order (refused while an earlier year still has income or expense balances: earlier_year_open), only a year that is over:

balances = per income and expense account, sum of lines before the year's end (non zero only)
total    = sum(balances)          (= −profit, since income is a credit)
lines    = each account: −balance ; 3401 Retained earnings: +total
AccountDebitCreditTags
each income accountits credit balance
each expense accountits debit balance
3401 Retained earningslossprofit

Key year_close:<year>; kind year_close; dated the year's last millisecond (Cairo). A lock past that moment is lifted for this entry and put back in the same transaction; the books are then closed through 31 December (or later if they already were). Profit and loss, profit per store and merchant profit leave the year_close entry out, so the closed year still shows its result. Book the company income tax (15.6) before closing.

15.17 Exports

Journal CSV

journalLines(db, { from, to }) returns every ledger line between two Cairo days, one row per line, ordered by entry number then amount (debits first). The CSV (GET /finance/export/journal.csv, apps/api/src/finance/period.controller.ts) is importable by Odoo, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Daftra:

Audit pack

GET /finance/export/audit-pack.zip (a stored zip, no library) holds, in a folder audit-<from>-<to>:

  • journal.csv: the journal above for the period;
  • trial-balance.csv: Account code, Account name, Debit, Credit for the period;
  • chart-of-accounts.csv: Code, Name, Name (Arabic), Category, Group, Active, Balance now (inactive accounts included);
  • vat-<year>.csv: Month, VAT on our fees, VAT claimed back, Due, Paid for the year of the period's last day;
  • receipts/JV-nnnnnn-k.jpg (or png, webp): every receipt photo attached to an entry, its request or its payout in the period, named by voucher.

All CSVs follow the house rules: UTF-8 byte order mark, formula prefixes neutralised, money as EGP text from integers.
