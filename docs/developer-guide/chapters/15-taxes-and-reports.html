15. Taxes, reports, reconciliation and closing

This chapter explains how the tax worksheets are built from the ledger (VAT, withholding, salary tax and insurance, company income tax), how tax is paid, how the financial statements and drill down reports compute and compare, how profit per store and the fully loaded cost per order share costs, how cash flow and the four week forecast work, how bank lines are matched, what the month end checklist checks, how a year is closed, and what the exports contain. Every report reads the ledger lines; nothing is stored as a balance.

15.1 Periods and Cairo time

Reports take Cairo calendar days. A day D covers [cairoDayStart(D), cairoDayStart(D + 1)); cairoDayStart() (packages/shared) applies Egypt's summer time, never a fixed offset. In SQL a moment's Cairo day or month is always taken as (occurred_at AT TIME ZONE 'UTC') AT TIME ZONE 'Africa/Cairo'. Drill down reports refuse ranges longer than 400 days (range_too_long).

15.2 VAT report (per month of a year)

vatReport(db, year) in packages/db/src/finance/period.ts reads every line on 2301 VAT payable in the year, by Cairo month. Columns follow the entry's kind, not only the line's sign:

purchase kinds = supplier_bill, supplier_bill_cancel, expense, expense_claim
output = −sum(lines) where kind = credit_note
                        or (kind not tax_payment, not a purchase kind, and line < 0)
input  =  sum(lines) where kind is a purchase kind
                        or (kind not tax_payment, not credit_note, not a purchase kind, and line > 0)
paid   =  sum(lines) where kind = tax_payment
due    = output − input

So a credit note lowers output, a cancelled bill lowers input, a debit note raises output, a rider office bill (kind supplier_bill) is input, and other entries (journal entries) go by sign.

15.3 Monthly VAT return

vatReturn(db, month) in packages/db/src/finance/returns.ts builds the return worksheet from the entries of the month that have a 2301 line (year close and tax payments excluded), grouped by kind:

for each kind: revenue = −sum(4101 lines); vat = −sum(2301 lines)
               expense = sum(lines on expense accounts, 2401 goods received and 2402 commission accrued)
credit_note        : creditNotes += −revenue ; creditNoteVat += −vat
purchase kinds     : purchases += expense + sum(data.onWalletPiastres) ; inputVat += −vat
revenue ≠ 0 or vat > 0 (fees, minimum, debit notes...):
                     sales += revenue ; outputVat += vat
                     (debit notes also shown apart as "of which")
otherwise          : purchases += expense ; inputVat += −vat
carriedIn = max(0, balance of 2301 before the month)        (a debit balance is credit carried in)
due       = outputVat − creditNoteVat − inputVat − carriedIn
duePiastres = max(due, 0) ; carriedOut = due < 0 ? −due : 0
paidSince = VAT tax payments booked after the month

A rider office's commission since 11 November 2026 is booked on the office wallet at each delivery, so its month end bill entry carries only the VAT; onWalletPiastres in the bill entry's data adds the commission back to purchases (counted once, since the entry has one 2301 line).

November: fees 400,000 EGP with VAT 56,000; one credit note of 1,000 EGP with VAT 140; bills and expenses with VAT claimed 9,000; a debit balance of 2,000 carried in from October. Due = 56,000 − 140 − 9,000 − 2,000 = 44,860 EGP; nothing carried out.

The month end checklist has a hand ticked item for the VAT return (15.15).

15.4 Withholding tax, form 41

withholdingPayments(db, { year, quarter }) lists every bill payment of the quarter that kept withholding tax, one row per payment, leaving out payments made by a cheque that later bounced or was cancelled (reversedPaymentIds()):

row: day, voucher JV number, supplier, tax id,
     net (amount before VAT of the payment = data.net = round(bill.net * settled / bill.total)),
     rate = net ? round(wht * 10000 / net) : supplier.whtBp,

The CSV (form41-<year>-Q<n>.csv) has the columns: Tax registration number, Name, Date, Amount before VAT, Rate % (two decimals), Withheld, Voucher. whtReport() in payables.ts gives the same quarter per supplier, flagging suppliers with withholding and no tax id.

15.5 Salary tax and social insurance lists

payrollForms(db, month) reads the month's payroll:<month> entry (chapter 14) and joins each person's national id and social insurance number from their employee file. Totals: gross, income tax, insured wages, both insurance shares, martyrs fund; missingIds counts people without an id or number. Two CSVs (salaries switch needed): the salary tax list and the social insurance list (Insurance number, National ID, Name, Insured wage, Employee share, Company share, Total).

15.6 Company income tax

corporateTaxEstimate(year):
    pl              = profit and loss of the year (year close entry left out)
    booked          = the 5801 Company income tax line of that year
    profitBeforeTax = pl.profit + booked

The accountant settles the amount (the taxable profit may differ from accounting profit). bookCorporateTax() books it before the year is closed, only for a year that is over, and only the difference with what is booked:

15.8 Financial statements

All three are in packages/db/src/finance/books.ts; they sum lines per account (sumsByAccount()) with optional store and merchant filters. A balance is shown "as an accountant reads it": natural(category, debit − credit) keeps the sign for assets and expenses and flips it for liabilities, equity and income.

ReportLines readResult

Accumulated depreciation (1701) is an asset account with a credit balance, so it appears as a negative asset. With a store or merchant filter only the lines tagged with them count, so a filtered report may not balance (the screen says so).

15.9 Drill down and comparisons

For the balance sheet the earnings drill starts after the last year close.

15.10 Profit per store and the fully loaded cost per order

profitBy(db, { from, to, by }) in packages/db/src/finance/profit.ts groups income (−sum of revenue category lines) and costs (sum of expense category lines) by the line's store, merchant or rider tag, year_close left out; lines without the tag form one company wide row.

costPerOrder():

orders(s)       = delivered orders of store s in the period (deliveredAt in range)
allOrders       = sum over stores
companyCosts    = costs of the untagged (company wide) row
shared(s)       = allOrders > 0 ? round(companyCosts * orders(s) / allOrders) : 0
incomePerOrder  = round(income(s) / orders(s))
directPerOrder  = round(directCosts(s) / orders(s))
fullCostPerOrder= round((directCosts(s) + shared(s)) / orders(s))
profitPerOrder  = round((income(s) − directCosts(s) − shared(s)) / orders(s))
(null when the store delivered nothing)

Store A delivered 3,000 orders, store B 1,000. Company wide costs 200,000 EGP. A's share = round(20,000,000 × 3,000 / 4,000) = 15,000,000 (150,000 EGP). A's direct costs 240,000 EGP: direct cost per order 80 EGP, fully loaded (240,000 + 150,000) / 3,000 = 130 EGP.

15.11 Cash flow (direct method)

cashFlow(db, { from, to, darkStoreId? }) in period.ts. Money is every line on groups 1100 (banks), 1200 (cash boxes, Money at Basata included) and 1300 (rider cash).

opening = sum of money lines before from ; closing = sum before to
per entry: net = sum of its money lines in the period
per kind : in  = sum of net over entries with net > 0
           out = −sum of net over entries with net < 0
section  : asset_purchase, asset_disposal → investing
           loan_received, loan_payment, capital_in, capital_out → financing
           every other kind → operating

Because each entry is netted first, a move between our own money accounts (a bank deposit from a box, a handover from rider cash to a box, a transfer between banks) nets to 0 and does not appear. Money borrowed and repaid is financing: a whole loan instalment, interest included, counts as financing (IAS 7 allows it); buying and selling equipment is investing; owner capital in and drawings out are financing; everything else is operating (SECTION in period.ts). With a store, only money lines tagged with that store count.

15.12 Four week cash forecast

cashForecast(db, now) in period.ts:

planned kinds = expense, bill_payment, payroll, tax_payment, staff_advance, prepayment,
                asset_purchase, capital_in, capital_out, other_income
trading       = sum(in − out) of moneyByKind over the last 56 days, planned kinds left out
weeklyTrading = round(trading / 8)
money now     = sum of money lines (1100, 1200, 1300) up to now
for week w = 0..3 (7 days from today):
    bills     = remaining of open bills due in the week (week 0 also takes overdue ones)
    recurring = amount of each active recurring expense whose day of month falls in the week
                (within its start and end months)
    salaries  = (number of month ends in the week) × gross of every package in force today
                for active employees on our payroll
    taxes     = week 0 only: VAT, withholding and postal fees owed now (never below 0)
    closing   = opening + weeklyTrading − (bills + recurring + salaries + taxes)

In week 0 the taxes also include the postal fees owed (2307): the query sums vat_payable, wht_payable and postal_fee_payable.

Forecast: 12 month scenarios

Accounting, Planning, Forecast holds named scenarios of the next 12 Cairo months, apart from the four week cash forecast above. Nothing in it posts to the ledger. Code: packages/shared/src/forecast.ts (the model, pure, whole piastres only, so the browser recalculates live while editing and the API gives the same numbers), packages/db/src/finance/forecast.ts (suggestion, storage, budget), apps/api/src/finance/forecast.controller.ts (/finance/forecasts).

Scenarios

A scenario (forecasts, numbers FC-000001 onward, gapless) has a name (2 to 80 characters), a start month (the current Cairo month when it was made), the months its suggestion read, the opening balances at the start of the first month, its drivers (forecast_drivers: month, key, value) and its line overrides (forecast_overrides: month, line, amount, note, who and when). Status draft or approved. createForecast() starts either from the system's suggestion or as a copy of another scenario (copyFromId: drivers, overrides, start month and opening copied). Drafts are edited (saveForecastDrivers(), saveForecastOverrides(), one transaction each, audited finance.forecast_edit), renamed or deleted; an approved scenario refuses edits (forecast_approved) until accounting manage opens it again (setForecastStatus(), audited finance.forecast_approve and finance.forecast_reopen). A month outside the scenario's 12 months answers month_outside_forecast, an unknown key invalid_driver or invalid_line. Up to 6 scenarios can be compared side by side (compareForecasts(): each month's orders, revenue, costs, profit and cash at the end).

The suggestion

forecastSuggestion() reads the last 3 complete Cairo months of the books (fewer when the books are younger, none before the first ledger line or delivered order) and writes drivers for the first month:

growth_bp          = average month on month change of delivered orders (months whose previous month had orders)
orders             = last month's delivered orders × (10000 + growth_bp) / 10000
orders_per_new_merchant = last month's orders / merchants with orders last month
cod_share_bp       = cash orders × 10000 / orders           cod_per_order = cash collected / cash orders
fee:<kind>         = fee income of that entry kind / orders  (per delivered order, before VAT)
rider_pay_per_order, office_commission_per_order, postal_per_order, packaging_per_order
                   = 5101, 5102, 5103, 5404 over the months / orders
salaries           = gross of the salary packages in force
rent, fixed:<account> = monthly average of every other income and cost account
                     (recurring expenses instead where set up; company income tax left out)
loan_principal, loan_interest, depreciation = from the loan schedules and the asset register
vat_bp             = fee.vat_bp            vat_input = average VAT claimed back per month
merchant_hold_days = days of last month's net cash the merchants' balance holds (at most the month's days)

Every division is mulDiv(a, b, c): a × b ÷ c rounded half away from zero, in whole numbers.

Drivers and the model

Level drivers (growth_bp, orders_per_new_merchant, cod_share_bp, cod_per_order, the four per order costs, salaries, rent, vat_bp, vat_input, merchant_hold_days, rider_cash_end, every fee:<kind> and fixed:<account>) keep their last set value in later months. Flow drivers (orders, new_merchants, capital_in, drawings, loans_in, loan_principal, loan_interest, capex, depreciation) count only in the month they are set. forecastCompute(), month by month:

base        = the month's own orders value when set, else (after month 1) base × (10000 + growth_bp) / 10000
newMerchants += new_merchants
orders      = max(0, base + newMerchants × orders_per_new_merchant)
codOrders   = orders × cod_share_bp / 10000 ;  codCollected = codOrders × cod_per_order
fee:<kind>  = orders × driver   ; rider_pay, office_commission, postal_fee, packaging = orders × per order driver
salaries, rent, fixed:<id>, depreciation, loan_interest = the driver
each line   = its override for the month when one is set, else the computed amount
profit      = revenue lines − cost lines ; feeIncome = sum of fee lines
VAT         vatOutput = feeIncome × vat_bp / 10000 ; last month's VAT owed (if positive) is paid this month;
            vatEnd = vatOwed − paid + vatOutput − vat_input   (a credit is carried, never paid back)
merchants   net = codCollected − feeIncome − vatOutput ; merchantsEnd = net × merchant_hold_days / days in month
            payouts = merchants opening + net − merchantsEnd
rider cash  = rider_cash_end, else the opening rider cash
operating   = profit + depreciation + Δmerchants + ΔVAT − Δrider cash
investing   = −capex
financing   = loans_in − loan_principal + capital_in − drawings
cash        += operating + investing + financing
balance sheet: cash, rider cash, equipment (+capex − depreciation), other assets (as opened) =
               merchants, VAT, loans, other liabilities (as opened) + capital (+capital_in − drawings) + retained (+profit)

Warnings name the months where cash goes below 0 (cash_below_zero), loans below 0 (loans_below_zero) or merchants owe us (merchants_owe_us). Periods are each month, the scenario's quarters (Q1 its first three months) and the year: flows add up, balances are those of the period's last month.

Overrides

Any profit and loss line of any month (a cost line, fee:<kind> or fixed:<account>, the account an income or expense account) can be set by hand with a note; the line is then marked edited and no longer computed. Clearing the override (null) computes it again. Each change is audited with the line, month, before, after and note.

Use as budget

forecastBudgetPreview() (accounting view) lists, per month and ledger account, what the scenario would write into the company wide budgets next to the budget there now: fee lines go to 4101, rider pay 5101, office commission 5102, postal fee 5103, packaging 5404, salaries 5402, rent 5401, depreciation 5601, loan interest 5904, fixed:<id> to its account. forecastToBudget() (accounting manage, only an approved scenario, else forecast_not_approved) writes them in one transaction through setBudget(), with mode replace (existing rows are overwritten) or skip (existing rows kept). A negative amount is never a budget and is skipped; system accounts not opened yet are opened. The result counts written and skipped rows; audited finance.forecast_to_budget. Rows written this way count as months set by hand (below). The screens are shown in the chapter Screens: Reporting and Planning, section "Forecast".

15.13 Budgets

Budgets are per income or expense account, month and store (or company wide); budgetVsActual() in period.ts shows the variance for a month (with a store: only that store's budgets and lines; without: the whole company).

Planning by quarter

Budgets are planned per quarter first (Q1 January to March, Q2 April to June, Q3 July to September, Q4 October to December), then by month; rows are still stored per month, so budget against actual per month keeps working (packages/shared/src/budgets.ts, packages/db/src/finance/budgets.ts, apps/api/src/finance/budgets.controller.ts).

splitQuarter(total) : each = floor(total / 3) ; months = [each, each, total − 2 × each]
                      (whole piastres; the last month takes the remainder)
setQuarterBudget(year, quarter, account, store or company wide, amount)
    → the three months get splitQuarter(amount), manual = false
setBudget(month, ...)  (the existing month route, PUT /finance/budgets)
    → that month only, manual = true
quarter amount = always the sum of its three months

Q1 rent of a store planned at 100,000.00 EGP (10,000,000 piastres): January 3,333,333, February 3,333,333, March 3,333,334. Changing March by hand to 4,000,000 marks it manual; the quarter now reads 10,666,666 and is shown as edited.

The account must be an income or expense account (account_not_found), the year 2020 to 2100 (invalid_year), the quarter 1 to 4 (invalid_quarter), the amount a whole number of piastres not below 0. Setting a quarter is audited as finance.budget_quarter_set with the months before and after; a month as finance.budget_set with its manual flag.

budgetYear(year, scope, groupCode?) (GET /finance/budgets/year?year=&scope=&group=) gives every income and expense account (active, or holding a budget) with budget, actual, variance and percent per month, per quarter and for the year. Scope all is every store and the company wide part together, company only company wide budgets and lines with no store, or a store id. Actual leaves the year_close entry out. The variance is positive when better than plan: actual − budget for income, budget − actual for costs; the percent is variancePercentBp(variance, budget) (none without a budget). A quarter is flagged edited when one of its months was changed by hand and uneven when its months are not the equal split.

copyBudgetYear(fromYear, toYear, scope, percentBp, replace) (POST /finance/budgets/copy) copies a year into another, optionally raised or lowered by a percent in basis points (500 = 5%; at least −10,000):

scaleBudget(amount, bp) = round half up(amount × (10000 + bp) / 10000), never below 0   (BigInt, no floats)
per account, store and quarter of the source year:
    no month changed by hand → splitQuarter(scaleBudget(quarter total, bp)), manual = false
    otherwise                → each month scaleBudget(month, bp), keeping its manual flag

It refuses same_year, budget_year_empty when the source year has nothing in the scope, and budget_year_not_empty when the target year already has rows in the scope unless replace is set, which deletes them first. Audited finance.budget_copy. Reading needs accounting view, setting and copying accounting use; people limited to some stores must name one of their stores. The screens are shown in the chapter Screens: Reporting and Planning, section "Budgets".

15.14 Bank reconciliation

Importing statements

Finance uploads the bank's statement as .xlsx or CSV (read on the server without a spreadsheet library; old .xls is refused with a "save as" hint), picks the columns (guessed from English and Arabic headers; debit and credit columns or one signed amount). Dates and amounts are parsed by parseStatementDate() and parseStatementAmount() (packages/shared/src/statement.ts: day first dates, month names, Excel day numbers; brackets, a minus or DR make an amount negative; no floats). Each upload is a statement BS-000001 onward. Each line gets a fingerprint:

base = day | amount | balance | description | reference
n    = how many times the same base appeared earlier in this file (1, 2...)
fingerprint = sha256(base | n)

Lines whose fingerprint already exists for the account are skipped, so overlapping uploads add nothing, while two equal transfers on one day in one file are both kept. A future day or an amount of 0 is refused.

Automatic matching

autoMatch() in packages/db/src/finance/bank.ts runs after an import and when the screens open. For each unmatched bank line, oldest first, it takes the ledger lines of the same account with exactly the same amount within 3 days that prove no bank line yet; when exactly one is the closest it is matched; two equally close ones are left for a person. Matching never records money.

Sorted by rank, then by distance from the bank amount, then by day gap, then by ids; at most 12 shown. Lines picked by hand match when they add up exactly (sumMatches(); the extra lines are kept in bank_line_matches; a trigger lets a ledger line prove only one bank line; reopening removes them). Money out with no entry can be booked as bank charges in one tap (bookBankCharge(): an expense on 5501 Bank charges through the second person rule, key expense:bankcharge:<bankLineId>, then matched); a line that needs no entry is set aside with a reason of at least 5 characters. Money in is never created from a statement line.

Reconciliation view

reconciliation(db, { accountId, day }): book balance at the end of the day, the last bank balance printed on a statement line up to that day, the difference, bank lines not in the books, and book lines not on any statement since the first day a statement covers.

Journal CSV

journalLines(db, { from, to }) returns every ledger line between two Cairo days, one row per line, ordered by entry number then amount (debits first). The CSV (GET /finance/export/journal.csv, apps/api/src/finance/period.controller.ts) is importable by Odoo, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Daftra:

Audit pack

GET /finance/export/audit-pack.zip (a stored zip, no library) holds, in a folder audit-<from>-<to>:

  • journal.csv: the journal above for the period;
  • trial-balance.csv: Account code, Account name, Debit, Credit for the period;
  • chart-of-accounts.csv: Code, Name, Name (Arabic), Category, Group, Active, Balance now (inactive accounts included);
  • vat-<year>.csv: Month, VAT on our fees, VAT claimed back, Due, Paid for the year of the period's last day;
  • receipts/JV-nnnnnn-k.jpg (or png, webp): every receipt photo attached to an entry, its request or its payout in the period, named by voucher.

All CSVs follow the house rules: UTF-8 byte order mark, formula prefixes neutralised, money as EGP text from integers.

The journal list and the entry page

The journal is browsed at /finance/journal, the Accounting list view journal (GET /finance/lists/journal, rows inside the person's stores). Search covers numbers, memos and references. Filters: dates (today and the other date presets; the default is the last 7 days), kind families (deliveries: the fee, COD, rider pay and postal fee kinds; cash; payouts: merchant payouts, rider bonus payouts and rider withdrawals; bills; manual: journal entries and adjustments), entries with an order, and entries without a receipt. Group by kind, day, month or who made it; sort by number, day or amount; the amount column is totalled. Every row opens /finance/journal/<id>, the entry page with its lines, source document, files and the printable voucher (chapter 12, "The journal entry page"). The screens are shown in the chapter Screens: Reporting and Planning, sections "The journal list", "Searching and filtering the journal" and "The journal entry page".
