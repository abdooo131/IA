Screens: Money out

This chapter walks through every screen of the Accounting app's Money out menu exactly as a user sees it: what is on the page, what each control does, the rule behind it, who may use it and what the result looks like. The menu holds, in this order: Record an expense, Suppliers, Bills (page title Supplier bills), Purchase orders, Expenses and transfers (page title Money movements), Petty cash, Rider withdrawals, Expense claims, Riders (page title Rider wallets), Rider offices, Payroll (page title Salaries) and Payslips, both only with the salaries switch, Recurring expenses and Equipment (page title Prepaid and equipment). Every page keeps its own address, so a link can be shared.

The screenshots come from the demo database. The demo team members' names are replaced by role labels (Finance User, Finance Approver, Store Staff A, Rider B and so on) and phone numbers are hidden. Red numbered circles on a screenshot point to the parts described in the legend table under it; they are not part of the app.

Rules shared by every Money out screen

  • Access. Every screen needs the Accounting app at view level. Saving anything needs use; a few decisions need manage (named per control below). People limited to some stores see only those stores' rows, boxes and riders. The API checks every call again (AccessGuard), so a hidden button is never the only protection: a refused call answers 403 no_access or store_not_allowed.
  • The approval limit. Money going out (expenses, bill payments, drawings, advances, prepayments, equipment, journal entries taking money out of a bank or box) is posted at once below finance.approval_threshold_piastres (default 10,000.00 EGP; 0 turns the rule off). From the limit it waits in Dashboard > Approvals for a second person. Money coming in (owner capital, other income, journal entries putting money into a bank or box) always waits while finance.approve_money_in is 1. Selling or scrapping equipment always waits. Nobody approves their own request (same_person). See the chapter Controls and access for the request kinds.
  • Money must exist. A bank or cash box never pays more than it holds at the moment of the payment or at any later moment (not_enough_money), and nothing is dated in closed books (books_closed) or in the future (future_date). See the chapter Expenses, suppliers, bills and the assets we pay for, rules shared by every movement of money.
  • Idempotency. Every save sends a fresh key made when the form opens, so a double tap or a retry posts once.
  • Amounts. Typed in EGP with up to two decimals (1250.50), sent to the API in piastres, never as floats.

The list view

Suppliers, Supplier bills, Petty cash, Rider withdrawals and Expense claims are list views (component ListView, see the chapter UI development). Their shared controls are: the search bar with facet chips (a light blue rounded chip such as "Filter Open ×"; the × removes it; Backspace in an empty search box removes the last chip), Filters (opens a panel of three columns: Filters, Group by, Favourites), Export (CSV or Excel of the rows passing the filters, or only the ticked rows), Columns (shows or hides optional columns, remembered per person), Per page (20, 50, 100), the count "1–5 of 5", a tick box per row and one in the header (select all), sortable column headers (click once ascending, again descending), a Total row summing money columns over every passing row, and cards instead of a table on phones. The list state lives in the address (f filters, g grouping, q search, sort, page), so the view can be bookmarked. Filters inside one group are joined by "or", groups by "and". Favourites saves the current search under a name ("Open the list with it" makes it the default).

Paying with withholding tax

The supplier keeps 1% withholding on the amount before VAT. Each payment keeps back its share: wht = net × rate × paid ÷ total, rounded to the piastre, and the last payment makes the total exact. Paying 3,000.00 of 6,840.00 keeps back 26.32 (6,000 × 1% × 3,000 ÷ 6,840) and 2,973.68 leaves CIB; paying the remaining 3,840.00 keeps back 33.68 and 3,806.32 leaves CIB; 60.00 in all, exactly 1% of 6,000.00. Each payment entry is Dr supplier wallet, Cr 2302 withholding tax payable, Cr the bank. See the chapter Expenses, suppliers, bills and the assets we pay for, bill payments, and Taxes for the quarterly form 41.

Selling or scrapping an asset

A disposal always waits for a second person (asset_disposal). On approval the cost and accumulated depreciation leave the books, the sale money comes into the bank, and the rest goes to 5902 (here a loss of 1,500.00: value 9,000.00, sold for 7,500.00). Months before the disposal month are charged first; a disposal in or before a month already charged is refused (disposal_before_charged_month).
