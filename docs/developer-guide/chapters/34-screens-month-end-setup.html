Screens: Month end, Setup and common parts

This chapter shows the screens under the Accounting menus Month end and Setup, the Accounting settings page, and then the common parts that every Accounting screen is built from: the list view, the document page frame, the status bar and the status pills, smart buttons, the chatter, the attachment field and viewer, the form footer and the settings menu. It ends with how the same screens look for a person with fewer rights, on a phone, in Arabic and in the dark theme.

Every screenshot was taken on the demo copy of the system on 11 November 2026 (Cairo), signed in as the owner unless the caption says otherwise. Names of demo staff and riders are blurred in the screenshots. Numbered red markers on a screenshot are added for this guide; they are not part of the screen.

Access words used in this chapter: view, use and manage are the levels a person holds on the Accounting app (user_access.accounting). The API decides every call: a GET needs view, any other call needs use, unless the route names another level (@Access('accounting', 'manage')). Owner means accounting manage plus settings manage (isOwner() in apps/web/src/lib/apps.ts). A page link is shown in a menu only to people who can open it.

Close the month (guided run)

Address: /finance/month-end/run?month=YYYY-MM&step=<item>. Menu: Accounting, Month end, Close the month. Also from the dashboard quick action Close the month and the dashboard tile Month end for ....

Who: anyone with accounting view opens it. Ticking an item by hand and untick need accounting use. Closing the books needs accounting manage. Code: apps/web/src/app/finance/month-end/run/page.tsx, components/finance/CloseMonthRun.tsx, lib/close-run.ts. Data: GET /finance/month-end?month= (the live checklist, monthEndChecklist in packages/db).

The run walks the month end checklist one item at a time. Without month it opens last month; without step it opens the first item that is not done (firstOpenStep), or the closing step when all are done. Nothing on this screen decides a rule; each item is computed live from the books every time the page loads.

Statuses and their colors

Accounting shows a status in three ways.

  • Status bar on a document page with a path (StatusBar, lib/workflows.ts): steps left to right in one white bar with grey borders. A done step has a ✓ and normal dark text; the current step has a Shiply Blue (#0046FC) ground with white bold text; later steps are grey (#5B6B82). A side state (cancelled, rejected...) is added at the end on a red (#B42318) ground with white bold text, and then no step is lit. On phones only the step before, the current one and the next show.
  • Status pill in lists and on documents without a path: a white pill with a grey (#DFE5EE) border and dark text, the same for every status. Exceptions: cheques (cleared in green, bounced and cancelled in red, the rest grey) and forecasts (Approved green on a light green tint, Draft grey on a light grey tint).
  • Text states: the month end checklist (Done green #0F6A35, n left and Tick when done orange #9A4100, After the month ends grey) and loans (Active or Repaid in grey under the title).

In the dark theme the same roles use lighter tones (for example green #5FD38D, orange #FFB35C) on Navy grounds.

Phone layout

On a phone (iPhone 13 width) the top bar keeps the grid button, the app name, a search icon, one Menu button for all the menus, and the gear. Lists become cards, and the document chatter moves below the content.

Arabic, right to left

With the language Arabic (cookie shiply_locale=ar) every screen mirrors: menus start at the right, the grid button and app name sit at the right, the gear and search at the left, and the chatter is on the left of the content. Numbers, codes and days stay left to right (ltr-iso). Text people typed (notes, memos) is shown as typed.
