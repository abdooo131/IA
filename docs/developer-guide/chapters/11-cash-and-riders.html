11. Cash on delivery, riders, store boxes and rider pay

This chapter follows the physical cash from the customer's hand to the bank, and the rider's earnings from the delivery to the withdrawal. It also covers what riders owe, Basata deposits, petty cash paid from store boxes and the rider supply offices. Amounts are integer piastres; share(a, bp) = floor((a * bp + 5000) / 10000) as in chapter 10.

11.2 COD collection

At delivery the rider enters the cash collected. The delivery transaction posts cod_collected (Dr 1300 rider cash, Cr 2100 merchant wallet, key delivery:<deliveryId>:cod, see chapter 10). When the collected amount differs from the expected COD a cod_mismatch alert is created; the posting always uses what was collected.

11.3 Rider cash limit at handover to the rider

Before a cash order is handed to a rider, checkRiderCashLimit() in packages/db/src/finance/cash-control.ts locks the rider row and checks:

cash     = max(0, balance(1300 rider cash) − pending Basata deposits)
carrying = sum(codExpectedPiastres) of the rider's deliveries in status assigned or picked_up
total    = cash + carrying + this order's COD
refuse with rider_cash_limit when total > finance.rider_cash_limit_piastres

Setting finance.rider_cash_limit_piastres: default 500,000 (5,000 EGP), store override, 0 turns the check off. Prepaid orders are not checked. A store manager (store manage) may hand it over anyway with a reason of at least 5 characters; the override is audited as order.rider_cash_override with the amounts. A frozen rider (11.8) gets no orders at all.

11.4 Cash age and the overdue alert

Cash is handed in oldest first, so the cash a rider still holds is made of the newest collections. heldPieces(balance, collections) in packages/shared/src/cash-control.ts walks the rider's positive 1300 lines newest first and takes from each until the balance is covered; any balance with no collection behind it counts as collected at time 0 (very old).

pieces = []
left = balance
for c in collections (newest first):
    take = min(left, c.amount); pieces.push(take at c.at); left −= take
if left > 0: pieces.push(left at epoch)

Buckets for Cash position: fresh (younger than finance.rider_cash_warn_hours, default 4), aging (up to the maximum), overdue (older than finance.rider_cash_max_hours, default 12, store override). The job checkRiderCashOverdue() runs every ten minutes and raises one rider_cash_overdue alert per rider and Cairo day (key rider_cash_overdue:<riderId>:<day>) when any piece is older than the maximum. Pending Basata deposits count as handed in for this check (they cover the oldest cash).

11.5 Cash handover to the store box

What the rider is expected to hand in

cashToHandIn() in packages/db/src/finance/operations.ts:

expected = max(0, balance(1300 rider cash)
                  − sum(differencePiastres of this rider's handovers still pending)
                  − sum(amountPiastres of this rider's Basata deposits still pending))

A pending difference stays in rider cash until finance decides, so it must not be expected again.

Recording the handover

Staff count the cash and enter the total. recordCashHandover() refuses when cash.store_handover_enabled is 0 for the store (store_handover_closed) or the rider belongs to another store, locks the rider row, computes difference = expected − counted and creates a handover row CH-000001 onward (gapless) with status balanced when the difference is 0, otherwise pending. The counted cash moves at once:

Idempotency key handover:<handoverId>; kind cash_handover. The staff action also carries its own client idempotency key, stored on the handover row.

Finance decides a difference

reviewCashHandover() takes a pending handover, a decision and a note:

  • write_off: the difference becomes a company loss (shortage) or gain (surplus).
  • charge_rider: a shortage moves to the rider's dues and is taken from their next withdrawal; a surplus is only marked (it stays in rider cash, no entry).

11.8 Basata deposits

Riders may deposit cash at a Basata branch instead of the store (packages/db/src/finance/basata.ts). Both channels can be closed per store: cash.basata_enabled and cash.store_handover_enabled (default 1, store override).

The rider sends a deposit

submitBasataDeposit(): amount, Basata reference (spaces removed, upper case, used once across all deposits), deposit time (not in the future, one minute of slack), the rider's own photo of the receipt (kind basata_receipt). Number BD-000001 onward. The most the rider may deposit:

max = cashToHandIn(rider) + deadline deductions not given back yet

Each deposit records the Cairo day whose deadline it counts for (deadlineDayOf()): the first deadline at or after the moment it was sent. No money moves until Accounting decides.

Accounting approves

approveBasataDeposit() (accounting use, never one's own). With held = rider cash less pending handover differences:

heldPart = min(amount, held)
rest     = amount − heldPart        (a late deposit of cash a deadline already took)
for each open deadline deduction, oldest first, while rest > 0:
    give = min(rest, deduction.amount − deduction.givenBack)
    if the deduction was charged to an office: credit the office wallet give
    else: credit 1350 dues up to what the rider still owes; the remainder credits 2200 wallet

                      − deposits sent before it and not decided before it)

Idempotency key basata_deadline:<riderId>:<YYYY-MM-DD>; kind basata_deadline_charge. A moment inside closed books is booked at the first open moment. The deduction is kept in rider_cash_deductions (amount and what later deposits gave back). The rider is frozen (reason deposit_deadline or deposit_rejected) and one rider_frozen alert is raised. The first run ever only marks its starting day and takes nothing. Each Cairo day is processed once (cash_deadline_runs); while the rule is off the days are marked skipped.

A frozen rider gets no orders at handover and cannot withdraw (rider_frozen), but can sign in and deposit. Only Accounting (accounting use) unfreezes, with a reason of at least 5 characters.

11.9 Rider pay per delivery

Riders earn only rider.bonus_per_delivery_piastres (default 0, store override, shown as "Rider pay per delivered order") per delivered order. It is posted at delivery (Dr 5101, Cr 2200 rider wallet, key delivery:<deliveryId>:bonus, kind rider_bonus; chapter 10). Failed attempts earn nothing. There is no salary, tax or insurance for riders paid per delivery; they are never on the salary run (employees.pay_basis = per_delivery).

11.10 The withdrawal hold

Each earning is on hold for rider.withdraw_hold_working_days (default 5, global) working days after its Cairo delivery day and is available from the start of the next day. availableFrom(day, rules) in packages/shared/src/rider-wallet.ts:

d = deliveryDay; left = holdDays
while left > 0:
    d = d + 1 day
    if weekday(d) in weekend or d is an official holiday: continue
    left −= 1
availableOn = d + 1 day           (with holdDays 0: the day after the delivery)

Weekend days come from rider.weekend_days (default 5): each decimal digit is a JavaScript weekday of the Cairo date, 0 Sunday to 6 Saturday. 5 is Friday, 56 is Friday and Saturday; Sunday is written last (50) because a leading 0 would be lost in the integer. Official holidays are HR's holiday rows. Weekdays are taken from the calendar day itself (new Date(day + 'T00:00:00Z').getUTCDay()).

Delivery on Wednesday 11 November 2026, hold 5, weekend Friday. Working days counted: Thu 12, (Fri 13 skipped), Sat 14, Sun 15, Mon 16, Tue 17. Available from Wednesday 18 November. If Sunday 15 November were an official holiday, it would be available from Thursday 19 November.

11.11 The wallet figures

Nothing is stored: riderWalletTotals() (packages/db/src/finance/rider-wallet.ts) reads the rider's 2200 lines (credits are earnings, debits are amounts paid out including deductions and old rider_bonus_payout entries), the dues (1350) and advance (1500) balances, and open withdrawals, then walletTotals() computes:

earnedAll = sum of all earnings
matured   = sum of earnings whose availableOn <= today
onHold    = earnedAll − matured
paid      = sum of debits on the 2200 wallet
owed      = max(0, dues) + max(0, advance)
pending   = sum(amount) of open withdrawals (requested or approved)
available = max(0, matured − paid − owed − pending)
balance   = earnedAll − paid

What the rider owes is netted before they ask, so the amount they ask for is what they receive.

11.12 Withdrawals

Request

requestWithdrawal() (the rider, from the rider app; idempotency key; number RW-000001 onward, gapless). It locks the rider and refuses: a disabled account; a frozen rider; more than rider.max_open_withdrawals open (default 1); more than available; less than rider.min_withdrawal_piastres (default 10,000) unless it is everything available. Methods: cash at the store, InstaPay (name@instapay or a mobile), mobile wallet (Vodafone Cash, Etisalat Cash, Orange Cash, WE Pay; an Egyptian mobile), bank (IBAN EG plus 27 digits). The owed amounts are set aside on the request, dues first, then the advance, never counting twice what other open requests already set aside:

setAside = sum(deduction) of other open requests
dues     = max(0, owedDues − setAside)
advance  = max(0, owedAdvance − max(0, setAside − owedDues))

Approve, reject, cancel

Accounting (accounting use, never the rider) approves; rejection needs a reason (requested or approved); the rider may cancel before approval. Rejection and cancel free the amount at once.

Pay

payWithdrawal() refuses the rider's own request, an unapproved one, and, from finance.approval_threshold_piastres (default 1,000,000; 0 turns it off), a payer who is also the approver (same_person). The source is the chosen open bank or box, else the store box for cash at the store, else CIB 1101; a store manager pays cash only from their store's box. The source row is locked and must hold the amount. Deductions are capped by what is still owed now; the wallet must still cover everything leaving it:

dues     = min(storedDues, owedDues now)
advance  = min(storedAdvance, owedAdvance now)
free     = matured − paid − (amount + deductions of the other open requests)
refuse more_than_available when amount + dues + advance > free

11.13 What riders owe (1350) and penalties

The 1350 rider dues wallet receives: shortages finance keeps on the rider (11.5), damage charged to the rider (chapter 10), deadline charges of riders without an office (11.8), and final violation deductions of riders paid per delivery. A staff advance given to a rider stays in 1500 and is netted the same way.

Penalty: chargeRiderPenalty() (job chargeRiderPenalties(), safe to run twice). For a final violation with a deduction:

amount = penalty == deduction_amount ? violation.amountPiastres
       : floor((round(days * 100) * rider.penalty_day_piastres + 50) / 100)
         (days come in quarters; worked in hundredths so no float reaches money)

rider.penalty_day_piastres: default 20,000, store override.

AccountDebitCreditTags
1350 rider duesamountrider, store
5101 rider payamountrider, store

Idempotency key violation:<violationId>:rider_penalty; kind rider_penalty. The violation is marked as taken (also when the amount is 0) so it never reaches the salary run.

Deduction of 1.25 days at 200 EGP per day: floor((125 × 20,000 + 50) / 100) = 25,000 piastres.

11.14 Rider supply offices

An office is a supplier with is_rider_office and its own commission_bp (accounting manage, audited). A rider paid per delivery links to one office through HR (employment outsourced, staffing supplier = the office). We pay the rider as usual; the office earns a commission on that pay. Code: packages/db/src/finance/rider-offices.ts.

Commission at each delivery

commission = share(riderPay, office.commissionBp)     (the rate at that moment)

Month end: shortages moved to the office, then the bill

"Prepare office bills" (accounting use) runs prepareOfficeBills() for a month that is over, in month order (later_month_prepared otherwise). For each office, under an advisory lock, dated the month's last second (or now when the books are closed by then):

  1. For each linked rider, what they still owed at the end of the month beyond everything their wallet held then moves onto the office: 
    duesAtEnd   = balance(1350) before the month end
holdsAtEnd  = 0 − balance(2200) before the month end
uncovered   = duesAtEnd − max(0, holdsAtEnd)
moved       = min(uncovered, dues now)     (only when > 0)
    Posting Dr office 2500 wallet, Cr 1350 rider dues; key office_shortage:<riderId>:<YYYY-MM>; kind rider_office_shortage.
  2. One bill per office and month (BILL- numbering), from the month's commission: 
    net      = sum of 5102 lines of the office's commission entries in the month
accrued  = part of it still sitting in 2402 (older deliveries)
vat      = office has a tax number ? share(net, fee.vat_bp) : 0
total    = net + vat
onWallet = net − accrued
