Questions merchants ask

Before you send us your first box

How much does it cost?

A fee per delivered order, a small share of cash collected, and storage per unit. No setup fee. We agree your rates before you start, and every fee shows on your statement.

What if an item is not in the store?

The 60 minute option does not show at checkout for that customer, so they are never promised what we cannot deliver.

What happens when the customer does not answer?

The rider calls, takes a photo at the door and brings the order back. We try again or return it to you, as you choose.

Who sets the delivery price my customer pays?

You do. The 60 minute price is yours and the money stays with you.

Do I need Shopify?

Shopify gives you the full connection. You can also create orders by hand from your dashboard.
