Address Tester

Type any Egyptian address in Arabic, English or Franco. It breaks it down and finds the area.

Results update as you type.

Paste one address per line, or choose a file: a text file, or a CSV (from Excel use "Save as CSV UTF 8"). In a CSV the address, city, province and area columns are joined.
